  • Foresight Moscow-2050

    The largest foresight study for the Moscow masterplan.

    I was part of the team that developed the methodology, strategy and overall structure of the study for the Moscow masterplan — a joint effort of three universities and research institutions and more than 2,000 experts from business, government and academia.

    The project shaped the long-term development strategy of the Moscow masterplan and made several landmark projects possible, including:

    • Autonomous taxis and delivery drones launched in real urban conditions together with Yandex and VkusVill.
    • Badaevsky — a one-of-a-kind residential complex raised 17 meters above the ground.
    • Moscow Regular River Transport — the launch and route planning of the city's regular river service.

    In collaboration with the Moscow Masterplan Institute and the Higher School of Economics

  • Nikolskiy market

    Redevelopment of a cluster of historic buildings in Saint Petersburg.

    The goal was to increase the value of a restored 40,000 m² historic building fivefold within five years.

    The overall development strategy was to create an attractive public space, fill it with hospitality operators, and acquire and redevelop the neighboring buildings in the block.

    As a result, Saint Petersburg gained another informal center of attraction in the heart of the city.

    In collaboration with Miles & Yards and Katarsis Architects

  • Aircraft manufacturing facility

    Development and optimization of the masterplan for a high-tech manufacturing center.

    The facility builds civil aircraft as well as fourth- and fifth-generation fighter jets.

    Our task was to expand and partially redesign the site to make room for new production facilities.
