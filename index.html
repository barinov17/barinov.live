Foresight Moscow‑2050

The largest foresight study for the Moscow masterplan.

I was part of the team that developed the methodology, strategy and overall structure of the study for the Moscow masterplan — a joint effort of three universities and research institutions and more than 2,000 experts from business, government and academia.

The project shaped the long-term development strategy of the Moscow masterplan and made several landmark projects possible, including:

  • Autonomous taxis and delivery drones launched in real urban conditions together with Yandex and VkusVill.
  • Badaevsky — a one-of-a-kind residential complex raised 17 meters above the ground.
  • Moscow Regular River Transport — the launch and route planning of the city's regular river service.

In collaboration with

  • Moscow Masterplan Institute
  • Higher School of Economics

Saint Petersburg masterplan

A strategy for an informal cultural center of the city, formed through three projects.

  • Sevkabel Port — a former cable plant on the Vasilievsky Island waterfront.
  • New Holland — a historic island in the city center.
  • Nikolskiye Ryady — a restored 40,000 m² historic market building.

At Nikolskiye Ryady the goal was to increase the value of the building fivefold within five years: create an attractive public space, fill it with hospitality operators, and acquire and redevelop the neighboring buildings in the block.
